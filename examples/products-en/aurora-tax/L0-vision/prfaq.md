# Aurora Tax — Press Release (internal, Working Backwards style)

**Belo Horizonte, MG — [launch date]** — Model firm Azevedo & Associados today announces Aurora Tax, a tax intelligence platform that answers questions about Brazil's Tax Reform (IBS/CBS) with verified regulatory grounding, in minutes instead of days.

Tax attorneys spend hours every week monitoring a continuous stream of regulations — complementary law, decrees, resolutions from the governing committee — and every client query requires manually reconstructing the applicable regulatory chain. Aurora Tax keeps the regulatory base alive, answers queries citing every applicable provision with a link to the primary source, and refuses to state anything it cannot substantiate.

"What changes is trust: the answer that reaches the client comes with an auditable basis, not the memory of whoever answered it," says the partner responsible for the practice area.

The product is available to tax law firms on a SaaS model.

## External FAQ

**Q: Does Aurora Tax replace the attorney's legal opinion?**
A: No. It produces the preliminary substantiation; the opinion is always reviewed and signed by an attorney.

**Q: How do I know the answer is correct?**
A: Every regulatory claim carries a clickable citation verified against the primary source. No source, no claim.
